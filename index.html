VAR replay + match recording for sports venues

Every match. Every moment. Replayed and shared.

smart-pitch brings professional-level video to any sports venue, from football pitches to tennis, padel, basketball and volleyball courts. Our cameras and software give players VAR-style instant replays during the game and record every match, so they can relive, edit and share their best moments afterwards.

VAR instant replay

Settle every close call on the spot. Review goals, line calls, fouls and match points from multiple angles while the game is still being played.

Automatic match recording

Your staff simply start and stop the recording. Our system takes over from there, capturing the match in high quality and preparing the video for players.

Share, edit & download

After the game, players get a shareable link to their match. They can trim highlights, edit their clips and download them to share anywhere.

smart-pitch was founded on a simple idea: the technology used in professional stadiums, from VAR to match video, should be available on every pitch and court. We set out to make it affordable and easy for any sports venue.

Today we supply, install and support both the camera hardware and the software, so venues have a single partner, and players get replays and videos that keep them coming back.
